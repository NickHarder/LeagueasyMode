How to use LeagueasyMode

A layer over League that shows what the scoreboard hides: timers, each enemy's likely gold, items, level and place, and how a fight would likely go. It reads only League's own local data on this Mac; it never reads the screen.

Starting

  1. Open the app: a scope icon appears in the menu bar, at the top right of the screen.
  2. Play League in Windowed or Borderless mode. The overlay follows League's window and shows once a game starts.
  3. The overlay never takes a click or a key from the game, except while you move the widgets (Edit layout).

Shortcuts

On a Windows keyboard, Ctrl is ⌃ Control, Alt is ⌥ Option and the Windows key is ⌘ Command.

Hold Tab or ⌥ Hold Tab or Alt Shows the details while held: win and fight chances, gold, the You panel, each enemy's second line, every callout and the spell buttons. The key still goes to League, so Tab shows its scoreboard too.
⌃⌥⌘R Ctrl + Alt + Windows + R Sends a problem report at once, without a note (see below).
⌃⌥1 to ⌃⌥5 Ctrl + Alt + 1 to Ctrl + Alt + 5 Picks an enemy, in role order: 1 top, 2 jungle, 3 mid, 4 bot, 5 support.

⌃⌥D Ctrl + Alt + D Within 3 seconds of a pick: that enemy used their other summoner spell.
⌃⌥R Ctrl + Alt + R Within 3 seconds of a pick: that enemy used their ultimate.

What the overlay shows

A number with ~ or a percentage is an estimate, worked out from what League shows; the rest is League's own. Each can be turned off in Settings.

  • Dragon: when the next dragon spawns, each team's dragons, and the rift's element.
  • Objective strip: Baron, Herald, grubs and their buffs, inhibitors coming back, turrets down per lane, and whether a team can likely contest an objective.
  • At a glance, with Details only while Tab or ⌥ is held on (the default): the timers, the newest two callouts that ask for something now (an objective soon, a numbers window, a laner missing, the jungler's start, an inhibitor open, a marked spell back, a suggestion) and one short line per enemy (role, name, R, level, respawn, the cooldowns you marked). Hold Tab or ⌥ for everything below, the other callouts included; while you are dead it all shows by itself. Turn the setting off to always see it.
  • Tab overview, while you hold Tab or ⌥ and while you are dead: both teams side by side, each player's rank, recent record, games and win rate on their champion, tags (one-trick, main, off-role, a streak) and item gold, and on the Rift each lane's item-gold lead between the two columns. Move it like any widget.
  • Enemy strip, a line per enemy: role, their ultimate's rank beside their name ("R2", or "no R" before level 6), level, items' gold, respawn and the cooldowns you marked. A second line shows only when it may change what you do: rank and record in the first 3 minutes, their next item when they can likely buy it, where the jungler or a roaming laner likely is, when they reach 6, 11 or 16, a trip to base. Detailed enemy rows in Settings shows every line all game, health, armor, magic resist and unspent gold included.
  • Win chance and fight: each team's chance to win the game, and to win a fight now, with what moves them.
  • You: what to buy next and your pace against your lane opponent.
  • Callouts: short notes when something changes, such as a numbers advantage or an objective soon. A level 6 or an item shows on the enemy's row, not as a callout; moments about friends and team calls are never on screen, only copied with ⌃⌥C. In a fight, news that can wait does, and the fight is called out once when it is over ("3 enemies down for 0:26"), not at each death. They can be spoken (Settings).
  • Minimap layer (with Minimap: jungler and roamers only in Settings, only the jungler and laners likely away from their lane), over League's minimap: a circle, with the first letters of the champion's name, where each unseen enemy likely is (larger and darker when likelier; none for an enemy your team sees), the camps down with their respawn, and the enemy's likely control wards.

On ARAM, its Mayhem, Arena and other maps no one has a role (no TOP, JGL and the rest), and the Rift's dragon, objectives, jungle and minimap layer hide, and so do the estimates of unspent gold, levels, trips to base and the win chance until they are tuned on those maps' games; the enemy strip and fights stay. Inhibitors coming back still show, marked ~ until their respawn time there is confirmed.

The menu

  • The first lines say which build this is, whether the engine runs, whether the overlay page loaded, and where League's window is.
  • Show overlay: as ⌃⌥⌘L.
  • Edit layout: the overlay turns tinted and takes the mouse, so you can drag a widget to move it. Drag its bottom right corner, or scroll over it, to make it bigger or smaller (60% to 160%). Esc ends it, and it ends by itself after 2 minutes. Reset layout puts every widget back in its usual place and size. Hide … on the same bar turns a widget off; Show …, or its Shown switch in Settings, turns it on again. League's own HUD is shaded red while you edit; a widget dropped on it moves just off it.
  • Window level: try another when the overlay does not stay above League.
  • Report a problem…: write what went wrong; see below.
  • Updates install by themselves when no game is on screen; the app restarts in a few seconds and the menu says "Update: build … installed". Turn off Install updates automatically to choose Install update yourself.
  • Open engine log, How to use…, Last game… (after a game: how it went, minute by minute), Settings… (what the overlay shows, and its Layout: sliders that place and size each widget exactly, fade their backgrounds so the game shows through, and fit the minimap layer over League's minimap) and Status… (what the engine sees, part by part).

When something goes wrong

  • The mouse or the keyboard does nothing in League: press ⌃⌥⌘L twice (hide, then show). If that does not help, quit the app from its menu; League works as before without it.
  • No overlay over the game: check that League is windowed or borderless, then the menu's first lines; try another Window level.
  • Anything else: use Report a problem… (or ⌃⌥⌘R in a game) right when it happens. The report holds the engine's log, the status page and the game so far, with every player's name taken out, and is sent on its own; just say "sent a report".
