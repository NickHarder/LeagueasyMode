# DR-04: Contest calls

No value yet (2026-10-29): no game the owner played has been scored; the first ones go with
build 184 or later, once installed.

- **Name:** contest calls.
- **The promise it proves:** UC-04, "can we take it".
- **Exact definition and formula:** the "objective contests" score of
  `src/leagueasymode/scoring.py`: the Brier score of the contest chance shown when a monster is up
  against whether the enemy fought for it, by the timeline, averaged over the games weighed by
  their objectives.
- **Unit and aggregation window:** a Brier score, over the last 20 Rift games.
- **Data source, and where it is computed:** [the accuracy score](/computations/accuracy-score.md)
  (`estimator=objective contests last=20`) over the accuracy history's "objective contests" lines.
- **Target, and the reasoning:** under 0.2, as for DR-03; an objective is fewer samples a game, so
  it takes more games to settle.
- **What gaming it looks like, and the guardrail:** as for DR-03, a constant 50%.
- **What decision changes when it moves:** up, refit the contest model; down, say "take it" at a
  glance (a suggestion) more often.
