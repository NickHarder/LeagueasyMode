# DR-02: The enemy jungler found

No value yet (2026-10-29): no game the owner played has been scored; the first ones go with
build 184 or later, once installed.

- **Name:** the enemy jungler found.
- **The promise it proves:** UC-02, "where is their jungler".
- **Exact definition and formula:** the "jungle path" score of `src/leagueasymode/scoring.py`:
  the mean distance between the camp the estimate names for each minute and the timeline's
  position of the jungler that minute, averaged over the games weighed by their samples.
- **Unit and aggregation window:** map units (the Rift is 14,800 across), over the last 20 Rift
  games.
- **Data source, and where it is computed:** the accuracy history's "jungle path" lines;
  `leagueasymode history` prints them.
- **Target, and the reasoning:** under 2,000 units, about a jungle quadrant: enough to say "top
  side" or "bot side", which is what a laner acts on. The threshold set from the first 20 games
  replaces this guess.
- **What gaming it looks like, and the guardrail:** naming a central camp every minute lowers the
  mean without saying anything; the positions' "chance on the truth" score, beside it in the
  history, falls when the estimate stops committing.
- **What decision changes when it moves:** up, refit the jungle path's priors (the usual start,
  the 4:00 habit) on the recorded games; down, show the jungler's place at a glance by default.
