# Metric

* [DR-01: Games scored](DR-01.md) - How many of the owner's games reach the accuracy history each week, recorded, sent and scored against the post-game timeline.
* [DR-02: The enemy jungler found](DR-02.md) - How far, in map units, the jungle path estimate puts the enemy jungler from where the post-game timeline shows them, on the Rift.
* [DR-03: Fight calls](DR-03.md) - How well the fight chance the overlay shows predicts the fights that followed, as a Brier score, on the Rift.
* [DR-04: Contest calls](DR-04.md) - How well the overlay's chance that the enemy can contest an objective predicts whether they did, as a Brier score, on the Rift.
* [GR-01: Engine stops](GR-01.md) - Crash reports the engine sends, per recorded game. A guardrail: the overlay that stops in a game is in the way.
* [GR-02: Problem reports](GR-02.md) - Problem reports the owner sends, per ten recorded games. A guardrail: the overlay in the way is what a report says.
* [NS-01: Games the overlay got right](NS-01.md) - The north star. The share of the owner's recorded games in which no estimator missed its accuracy threshold, the engine did not stop, and no problem was reported.
