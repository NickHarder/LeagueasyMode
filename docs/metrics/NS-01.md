# NS-01: Games the overlay got right

No value yet (2026-10-29): no game the owner played has been scored; the first ones go with
build 184 or later, once installed.

- **Name:** games the overlay got right.
- **The promise it proves:** P-01's success, "they make a better call in the game because the
  overlay told them something true they could not see, and it never got in the way"
  ([USERS.md](../USERS.md)): true is the drivers' accuracy, never in the way the guardrails'.
  Use cases UC-02, UC-04, UC-05, UC-06 and UC-09.
- **Exact definition and formula:** over the recorded games in the window, the share in which
  (a) every estimator scored on the game met its threshold in `tests/accuracy_thresholds.json`
  (an estimator without one counts as met), (b) no crash report has a time inside the game, and
  (c) no problem report was made during or within ten minutes after it.
  `NS-01 = games meeting (a), (b) and (c) / games recorded`.
- **Unit and aggregation window:** a share of games, over the last 20 games recorded.
- **Data source, and where it is computed:** the accuracy history (`accuracy-history.jsonl`,
  `src/leagueasymode/accuracy_history.py`) for (a); the recordings repository's `games/`,
  `crash-reports/` and `reports/` for (b), (c) and the count. Its attested computation, which
  joins the two by time, is to write with the first scored games; until then it has no value.
- **Target, and the reasoning:** 0.8 once thresholds exist. The thresholds are set from the first
  20 games at each estimator's average less one deviation (phase 6.2), so about 5 in 6 games meet
  each one; with a few estimators and a little instability, 4 in 5 games right is the bar.
- **What gaming it looks like, and the guardrail:** lowering a threshold (never done: phase 6.2
  forbids it), or recording fewer games (DR-01 falls), or not reporting problems (GR-02 is read
  beside the owner's own messages).
- **What decision changes when it moves:** down, stop adding and fix the estimator, the crash or
  the report behind the missed games; up and steady, build the next estimator or slice.
