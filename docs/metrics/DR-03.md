# DR-03: Fight calls

No value yet (2026-10-29): no game the owner played has been scored; the first ones go with
build 184 or later, once installed.

- **Name:** fight calls.
- **The promise it proves:** UC-05, "fight or not".
- **Exact definition and formula:** the "fights" score of `src/leagueasymode/scoring.py`: the
  Brier score of the fight chance shown before each fight against whether the player's team won
  it, by the timeline's kills, averaged over the games weighed by their fights.
- **Unit and aggregation window:** a Brier score (0 is perfect; 0.25 is a coin), over the last 20
  Rift games.
- **Data source, and where it is computed:** the accuracy history's "fights" lines.
- **Target, and the reasoning:** under 0.2: better than a coin by enough that a 70% call is worth
  acting on. The model is hand-set until refit (phase 5.5).
- **What gaming it looks like, and the guardrail:** saying 50% every time scores 0.25 and is never
  wrong enough to notice; the target sits below that, and the refit uses the same games.
- **What decision changes when it moves:** up, refit the fight model (`leagueasymode refit`);
  down, show the fight chance at a glance in a fight.
