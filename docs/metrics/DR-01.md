# DR-01: Games scored

Measured 2026-10-29: 1 game sent on 2026-10-28 and 29
([the recordings counts](/computations/recordings-counts.md),
`since=2026-10-28 until=2026-10-30 metric=games`, attested). It is a Practice Tool game ended at
0:00, so 0 scored. The owner's ARAM and Swiftplay games of those days were left open on the Mac
(fixed in #89).

- **Name:** games scored.
- **The promise it proves:** UC-08, and every other metric: without a game recorded, sent and
  scored there is no story to tell after it and no accuracy to measure.
- **Exact definition and formula:** the number of games in `games/` of the recordings repository
  whose recording started in the week, and of those the number with a line in the accuracy
  history.
- **Unit and aggregation window:** games a week (Monday to Sunday, UTC); both counts.
- **Data source, and where it is computed:** the recordings repository's `games/` (file names hold
  the start time), counted by [the recordings counts](/computations/recordings-counts.md), and the
  accuracy history; the status page's "Sending games" says the count sent
  on the Mac.
- **Target, and the reasoning:** 10 a week. The owner plays "a ton" in a session; 10 a week gives
  the 20 games phase 6.2 needs for thresholds within a fortnight.
- **What gaming it looks like, and the guardrail:** recording Practice Tool games or games left at
  0:00 to fill the count; the scored count, which needs the post-game timeline of a real match,
  does not count them.
- **What decision changes when it moves:** at 0 for a day the owner played, recording or sending
  is broken: that is the first thing to fix, before any estimator.
